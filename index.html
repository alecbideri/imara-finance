Questions

What borrowers ask us

Who can apply?+

Salaried workers, parents with school invoices, small businesses and anyone facing an emergency. The final yes comes after reviewing your documents, usually on a short call or visit.

What do I need to bring?+

National ID plus one supporting document: salary slip, business registration or stock proof, school invoice or an equipment quote. Originals are best when you visit.

How fast is an emergency loan?+

Emergency cases are treated first. For every product we confirm the current timeline on 0781600088 before you prepare documents.

How are my installments decided?+

From the amount and the term you agree. You see the full schedule in writing before you sign, with no hidden extra.

Can I pay early or top up?+

Yes, on request. Mention it in your message or at the office and we confirm how it works for your plan.
